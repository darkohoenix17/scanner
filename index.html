Background scanner (alerts when this page is closed)

A free Cloudflare Worker scans your watchlist every minute, 24/7, and sends Telegram alerts. It also unlocks forex, indices, stocks and futures data.
Background timeframes
How to set it up (about 10 minutes, once)
  1. Sign up free at dash.cloudflare.com. Go to Workers & Pages, then Create, then Hello World. Deploy it, tap Edit code, delete everything, paste the whole worker.js file, and Deploy.
  2. Storage & Databases, then KV, then Create a namespace named sharpscan.
  3. Open your worker, Settings, Bindings, Add, KV namespace. Variable name: KV. Pick sharpscan.
  4. Settings, Variables and Secrets, Add: name KEY, value = a password you choose.
  5. Settings, Trigger Events, Cron Triggers, Add: * * * * * (every minute).
  6. Copy the worker’s workers.dev URL and paste it above with your password.
